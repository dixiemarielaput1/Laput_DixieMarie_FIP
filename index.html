BILLY BEER STORY

Billy Beer, first brewed in the 1970s, is a testament to Canadian brewing history. Named after Billy Carter, and charismatic personality.

Adorned with a distinctive emblem of a coiled rattlesnake, symbolizing Billy's Canadian heritage and fearless demeanor, Billy Beer quickly captured the imagination of collectors and beer aficionados alike.
